JaffaXPress Removals offers friendly, reliable home removals, furniture deliveries and clearance services across London and Sussex, with longer distance moves available by arrangement. We operate a Luton van with a tail lift and can assist with loading, unloading, packing and furniture dismantling by arrangement. Whether you need help with a full home move, a single bulky item or selected clearance work, we’ll handle your belongings with care and aim to make the process straightforward. We’re registered as an Upper Tier Waste Carrier for eligible clearance work.
