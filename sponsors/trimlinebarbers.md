Trimline Barbers brings professional, convenient barbering directly to your doorstep. Serving East London, we offer quality haircuts, beard grooming and children's cuts, making it easier for busy individuals and families to look and feel their best.

But we're about more than haircuts. Through our Confidence, Passed On initiative, £5 from every eligible paid haircut supports Restoring Confidence CIC, helping provide free barbering services to people experiencing homelessness and hardship.

Your haircut helps pay for someone else's.

Book with Trimline and be part of something bigger.
